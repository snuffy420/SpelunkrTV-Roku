SpelunkrTV Guide for Roku

privacy UNOFFICIAL APP

How the Spelunkr TV Guide Roku app handles your information. Last updated November 11, 2026 · app version 1.6.

The short version: the app has no accounts, no sign-in, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. It does not collect, sell or share personal information, and the developer receives nothing about you from it.

What the app does not collect

  • No account, name, email address or profile.
  • No location data, contacts, camera, microphone or files.
  • No advertising identifiers, analytics events, crash reports or usage statistics.
  • No cookies or tracking pixels, and no data sold or shared with anyone.

Network requests the app makes

To show listings and play video, the app requests public data directly from two places:

  • Spelunkr for the channel lineup, schedules and program details.
  • Internet Archive (archive.org) for the video stream of each program.

As with any request on the internet, those servers see your IP address and standard request information (such as the type of device or app making the request), and they handle it under their own policies. The developer of this app does not receive, log or store that information.

Roku's platform may collect its own device and usage data under Roku's privacy policy. That is outside this app's control and is not something the developer can see.

What is stored on your device

The app may keep small preferences, such as your last channel or position in the guide, in local storage on your Roku. These stay on the device, are never uploaded, and are removed when you delete the app or clear its data.

Content and sources

Spelunkr TV Guide is an independent, fan-made client. It is not made, endorsed or supported by Spelunkr or by the Internet Archive.

  • Channel names, logos, descriptions and schedules come from Spelunkr's public guide data and belong to Spelunkr and its sources.
  • All programming is streamed directly from the Internet Archive. The app hosts no video, keeps no copy of it, and does not record, download, alter or redistribute it.
  • Copyright and other rights in the programs, films and commercials remain with their respective owners. This app claims no rights to that content.
  • Programs can become unavailable or change without notice. The app does not guarantee that a listing is accurate or that a stream will play.

If you believe content reachable through the app infringes your rights, contact the Internet Archive as the host of the material, or contact the developer about the app itself.

The contact form

The contact page is a separate website, not part of the Roku app. If you choose to use it, the name, email address and message you enter are delivered to the developer through Formspree and are used only to reply to you. Nothing is sent unless you submit the form, and you can ask for your message to be deleted at any time.

Donations

Donation links open in a browser and are entirely optional. The app never asks for payment details. If you send Bitcoin, the transaction is public on the blockchain by design; the developer does not link it to your identity.

Children's privacy

The app is not directed at children and collects no personal information from anyone, including children under 13. There are no accounts, chat features, purchases or advertising.

Changes to this policy

If the app's data handling changes, this page will be updated and the date above revised. The current version always applies to the latest release.
